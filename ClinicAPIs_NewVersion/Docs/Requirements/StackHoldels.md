## 4. Stakeholders & Actors

### Patient
The patient uses the system to manage appointments and access their medical information.

### Doctor
The doctor uses the system to manage appointments, review medical records, record diagnoses, and prescribe treatments.

### Nurse
The nurse uses the system to manage assigned patients and record examination results.

### Receptionist
The receptionist manages patient registration and appointments.

### Accountant
The accountant manages financial operations, payments, and employee salaries.

### Manager
The manager manages users, staff, and system access.
