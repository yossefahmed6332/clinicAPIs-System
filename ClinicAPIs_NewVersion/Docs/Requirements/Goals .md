## 3. Goals & Objectives

- **Patient Management:** The system should provide efficient management of patient information, medical history, and appointments.

- **Medical Staff Management:** The system should support the management of doctors, nurses, and other medical staff, including their profiles, availability, and assigned responsibilities.

- **Appointment Management:** The system should provide an organized process for booking, rescheduling, canceling, and managing appointments based on doctor availability.

- **Medical Record Management:** The system should allow authorized medical staff to securely store, access, and update patient medical records.

- **Medical Examination Management:** The system should allow medical staff to record examination results and enable doctors to use these results when diagnosing patients and prescribing treatments.

- **Financial Management:** The system should support the management of clinic billing, payments, financial operations, and employee salaries.

- **User & Access Management:** The system should allow authorized managers to manage system users and control access to sensitive information based on user roles.

- **Reporting & Analytics:** The system should provide reporting and analytics capabilities to help clinic management make informed decisions based on operational data.
- 
-**Cleaning & Maintenance Management:** The system should support the management of cleaning schedules and protocols to ensure the cleanliness and hygiene of the clinic premises.
