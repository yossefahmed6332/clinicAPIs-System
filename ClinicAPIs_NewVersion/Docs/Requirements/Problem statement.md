##Problem statements
#Patient challenges : 
- book appoitment and find available doctor 
- came a doctor with a medical Record and get the Prescription after get the medical examination 
-Cancel appointments.
- sign up or log in 

#Doctor challenges : 
-find the appointments of the day 
-know the medical records of the patients he will meet
-add diagnosis 
-add Prescription
-get the answers of medical examination 

#Accountant challenges:  
-Create operation 
-Check operation 
-calculate the monthly income ( from each doctor in clinic ) 
-salary employee 

#manager challenges : 
-reach every thing in system exclude operation and sensitive user info 
- Create User (doctor, accountant, employee)
- delete user (doctor, accountant, employee) 

#Receptionist challenges
-Register new patients.
-Book appointments for patients.
-Find available doctors.
-Reschedule appointments.
-Cancel appointments.
-Check patient appointments.
-Check patient arrival and registration.


#Nurse challenges
-View the appointments of the day.
-Access the medical records of assigned patients.
-Record the patient's vital signs and examination results.
-Prepare the patient for the doctor's examination.
-Update the patient's medical record with examination results.
-Assist the doctor during the examination.

#Cleaner challenges
-Ensure the cleanliness and hygiene of the clinic premises.
-Maintain cleaning schedules and protocols.
